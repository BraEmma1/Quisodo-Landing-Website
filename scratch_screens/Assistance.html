Frequently Asked Questions

Details regarding your upcoming sanctuary experience. If your inquiry is not addressed here, please contact our concierge.

Reservations & Arrival

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, allowing us to ensure your sanctuary is flawlessly prepared. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out may be arranged subject to availability and a supplementary fee. We invite early arrivals to leave their luggage with our concierge and enjoy the riverside lounge.

We offer private, chauffeur-driven transfers from the nearest international airport. Alternatively, for a truly spectacular arrival, helicopter transfers directly to our riverside helipad can be arranged. Please contact our reservations team at least 72 hours prior to arrival to secure your preferred method of transit.

Experiences & Amenities

Yes, our fleet of luxury vessels is available for private charter. Whether you desire a serene sunset cruise with champagne, a guided historical tour along the Volta, or a secluded picnic on a private sandbank, our captains and crew will curate a bespoke river experience.

The river teems with life, offering exceptional sport fishing. We provide guided excursions with expert local anglers, catering to both novices and seasoned fishermen. Top-tier equipment is provided, and our culinary team can prepare your day's catch to your exact specifications for evening dining.

Policies

To ensure the exclusivity of our sanctuary, cancellations must be made at least 14 days prior to your scheduled arrival to receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 14 days will forfeit the deposit. Certain peak seasons or exclusive buyout reservations may have stricter, non-refundable terms.

To maintain an atmosphere of profound tranquility and quiet luxury, Quisodo Riverside Sanctuary is an adult-only retreat (guests must be 18 or older). Furthermore, to protect the delicate local ecosystem and ensure the comfort of all guests, pets are respectfully declined.
